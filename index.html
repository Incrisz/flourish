Who am i?

I'm Flourish Godwin, a video editor and content creator

I'm a creative video editor with a passion for turning raw footage into compelling stories that connect. With a strong background in editing podcast clips, short-form content, and lifestyle/documentary videos, I bring a sharp eye for pacing, emotion, and rhythm to every project. Whether I'm crafting a mini-doc, editing a podcast, or cutting lifestyle videos for YouTube, I focus on clean visuals, clear pacing, and storytelling that resonates. I'm skilled in tools like Premiere Pro, CapCut, DaVinci Resolve and Canva.
